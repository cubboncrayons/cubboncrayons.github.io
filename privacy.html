Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026. Cubbon Crayons publishes printable Kannada–English stories and activities for adults to use with children. This notice covers the current informational website; checkout services have their own notices.

Information processed

The static catalogue does not require an account and does not itself operate a contact form, advertising pixels, visitor analytics, or behavioural profiling. GitHub Pages may collect server request data such as IP addresses and technical metadata to operate its hosting service. We do not knowingly solicit names, photos, or other personal details about children.

Orders and communications

If you choose to contact us through Payhip support or Instagram, the chosen platform processes information under its own privacy terms. If a purchase link is enabled, Payhip or the specified checkout provider may collect contact, billing, order and payment information to process your order and provide the digital download. Do not send payment card numbers or passwords to us by message.

Purpose, sharing and retention

We use information voluntarily sent to us only to answer enquiries, fulfil customer support, investigate complaints and meet lawful obligations. We do not sell customer information. Platform providers process technical and transaction data under their own agreements. We retain business communications and transaction records only for necessary support, disputes, accounting or legal duties, and review them for deletion when no longer needed.

Cookies, third-party links and security

Our current catalogue code does not set first-party tracking cookies or send analytics events to an external analytics service. Some links to Payhip include campaign labels such as utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign to describe that the visit originated from the Cubbon Crayons website. These labels contain no name, email or child information. Payhip or another external service may process referral and purchase data under its own privacy notice after you follow a link. External websites may set their own cookies. We take reasonable steps to limit information collection; no online service is completely secure. See GitHub's privacy statement and the applicable Payhip and Instagram notices.

Privacy rights and children

Adults may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information they have provided to us, subject to lawful retention requirements. Our products are for children to use offline with an adult; the online storefront is directed to adults. If future site features require children's personal data, we will assess applicable parental-consent and child-protection requirements before introducing them.

Privacy contact

Contact through Cubbon Crayons support on Payhip. A dedicated privacy/grievance email and verified operator details will be added before paid sales are activated. This policy will be updated if our processing activities change.
