## Analytics without unnecessary data collection
- Product links include non-identifying campaign parameters; these are attribution *labels*, not live conversion analytics.
- If Payhip offers reports of referring URL or UTM attribution, confirm and record them; otherwise use a privacy-reviewed analytics service and update CSP and the privacy notice accordingly.

## Launch readiness
A 9+/10 launch requires: accurate offers, real tested checkout and delivery, accessible responsive pages, privacy-aligned attribution, credible support information and genuine consumer evidence. No code-only rating can substitute for these tests.
