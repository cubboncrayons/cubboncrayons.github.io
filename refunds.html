Refund, Cancellation and Delivery Policy

Effective: 16 November 2026. Cubbon Crayons offers printable digital PDF products; no physical product is shipped unless a listing explicitly says otherwise.

Before payment

Each active listing should identify the story title, file type, language, page count, suitability, price and payment/delivery method before you pay. Buyers must actively confirm a purchase through the checkout provider. The Cubbon Crayons website links to external Payhip product pages. It does not process card or UPI payments itself. Availability and accepted payment options depend on the actual Payhip checkout.

Digital delivery

For a successfully completed order on an active Payhip listing, the provider normally supplies a download link or access instructions through its order confirmation page and/or purchase email. Delivery is electronic; shipping fees and physical delivery do not apply to PDF-only purchases. Actual availability depends on the checkout provider and listing.

Cancellation and refunds

Digital files can be copied after delivery, so change-of-mind cancellations generally cannot be accepted after successful access or download. Before a file is delivered or accessed, contact support promptly to request cancellation. This is not a waiver of consumer rights. If a file is not delivered, cannot be opened, is materially corrupted, substantially differs from its description, or the payment was duplicated or unauthorised, contact us with the order reference. We will investigate and offer a replacement, corrected file or refund as appropriate under applicable law.

How to request help

Please contact @cubboncrayons with the product title, purchase date, order reference and a description of the issue. Do not share full card numbers, passwords or sensitive payment details. We aim to acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within one month, with an earlier remedy where feasible. Our dedicated complaint email and verified grievance-officer details have not yet been published; this is an outstanding operator action. See Business & Support for the current contact method.

Refund processing

Approved refunds are ordinarily initiated through the original checkout/payment provider to the original method of payment, subject to its processing timelines and banking network. Statutory rights and remedies prevail over any conflicting provision of this policy.

Customer remedies

You may also contact the National Consumer Helpline or another lawful dispute-resolution body. Nothing in this policy removes any rights available under Indian consumer protection law.
